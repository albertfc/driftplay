Important limitations

  • The HLS manifest and all audio segments must allow browser CORS requests.
  • GitHub Pages cannot bypass CORS because DriftPlay has no backend proxy.
  • The browser tab must stay open for live media ingestion to continue.
  • Browser memory, Media Source Extensions, hls.js eviction, and the origin playlist window limit retained audio.
  • Paused positions can expire, and the buffer is not persistent across reloads.
  • Autoplay policies may require clicking Play before audio starts.
  • HLS audio codec support varies by browser; streams with video or unsupported codecs may fail.
